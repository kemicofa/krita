Ten original pencils for sketching in Krita, including graphite, mechanical,
carpenter, charcoal, and Conté styles. Nine pencils respond to pen tilt with a wider,
flatter contact; all ten respond to pressure. Original paper textures and brush tips
are embedded in every preset.

Download **Tilt_Sketch_Pencils.bundle** and import it through **Settings → Manage
Resource Libraries → Import**. Search your brush presets for **TSP**.

The ZIP includes the bundle, preview sheet, guide, brush catalog, license, and test
report. Pressure and tilt were checked through actual Krita rendering with synthetic
tablet events; physical tablet feel has not been tested. A tilt-capable pen/tablet
is needed for the tilt effects.
